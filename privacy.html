Reserv Flight Privacy Commitment

Reserv Flight (“Reserv Flight”, “Company”, “Us”, or “We”) understand the importance of protecting your personal information and are committed to maintaining your trust. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, protect, maintain, and share your information when you visit the website https://reservflight.online/ and mobile services of the company.

Consent

By using our website or any services, you agree to receive notifications and messages from Reserv Flight regarding your reservation and fare deals as per the policy given below. You must read and understand the Privacy Policy followed by the Terms and Conditions.

What Personal Information Do We Collect?

Information which you provide while subscribing to or registering on the Website, including but not limited to information about your personal identity such as name, gender, marital status, age, etc., your contact details such as your email address, postal address, frequent flyer number, and telephone (mobile or otherwise) numbers.

• Full Name & Contact Info
• Email ID & Phone Number
• Permanent & Current Address
• Passenger DOB, Gender & Special Needs
• Credit/Debit Card Details
• Travel Dates (Departure & Arrival)
• Destination & Travel Preferences
• Device ID & Flight Itinerary Details

How Do We Collect Your Personal Information?

  • When you fill out an online flight booking form or contact us on our website.
  • Using cookies and tracking technologies to reflect your browsing behavior.
  • Through interactions on social media platforms or shared content.
  • From third-party providers including travel partner networks.

Log Files

Reserv Flight follows standard procedures regarding Log Files. These log files collect Internet Protocol (IP), Internet Service Provider (ISP), Browser type, date and time stamp, exit pages, and click counts. These log files are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely to analyze trends and administer the website.

Children Policy (Age Restriction)

Our website is intended for individuals aged 18 or older and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has submitted information, contact us immediately at info@reserveflight.online and we will delete it promptly.

Security of Data

We handle and collect all personal information securely using industry-standard security measures. Payment transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology to ensure maximum protection.

CCPA Privacy Rights

California consumers have the right to request disclosure, deletion, or restriction of personal data under the CCPA.

GDPR Protection Rights

Users have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of personal data under GDPR regulations.
